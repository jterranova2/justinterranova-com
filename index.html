02

How I work

Three steps, in this order. Each one pays for itself before the next one starts.

  1. 1

    Structure the data

    I pull the exports, spreadsheets and system tables into one clean model, with definitions everyone signs off on. It's the unglamorous part, and it's the reason the rest holds up.

    You get One set of numbers the whole team agrees on

  2. 2

    Build what people use

    Reports and tools shaped around the meeting they're for: the Monday ops review, the shift plan, the month-end close. If a supervisor won't open it, it doesn't count.

    You get The manual weekly report, retired

  3. 3

    Add the AI layer

    Once the numbers are right, a manager can ask a question in plain English and get a straight answer, or read a short written summary each week instead of digging through a spreadsheet.

    You get Answers you can act on, Monday morning
